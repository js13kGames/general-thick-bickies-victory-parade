The long war is over. Humanity has been defeated!

Unicorns control the glitter now. **All. Of. It.**

Despite losing a leg in the war, you are still an extremely capable unicorn and have been given the honour of victory parade decoration duties.

Help to prepare for General Thick Bickies Victory Parade!

## How To Play
- At the start of each round you will be given a list of decorations and where to put them.  
- You have **15 seconds** to apply them.  
- Use your hoof to select a decoration and then place them according to the list.  
- Brigadier Sugar Cube Serenade will review your work.  
- Bad decorations will not be tolerated by the Brigadier. *A bad parade will humiliate General Thick Bickies* and there will be consequences. 
- You don't want to end up in **the Glitter Mines** do you?!

## Controls - Desktop only - Mouse and Keyboard
1. **LEFT** click on a crate or pot to select that decoration.
2. **LEFT** click to apply or place the decoration according to the list.
3. **SPACE** bar rotates rainbows 90 degrees at a time before placement
4. **HOLD** down **LEFT** click to apply paint.
